Self-hosted web fonts (latin subsets, WOFF2), unmodified copies of the files
Google Fonts served to this app before they were moved in-repo.

- Sora (variable, weights 100-800) — Copyright 2018 The Sora Project Authors.
  SIL Open Font License 1.1. https://github.com/sora-xor/sora-font
- IBM Plex Sans (variable, weights 400-700) and IBM Plex Mono (500, 600) —
  Copyright IBM Corp. 2017. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/IBM/plex

The OFL permits bundling and redistribution with software; the fonts
themselves are not sold. Declarations live in src/fonts.css.
